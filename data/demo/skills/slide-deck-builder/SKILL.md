# Slide deck builder

Build a deck that carries an argument, not a list of bullet points.

## Structure

1. One sentence stating the decision or claim.
2. The evidence, one idea per slide.
3. The counter-argument and why it does not change the conclusion.
4. The ask.

## Layout rules

Use one layout per slide type and do not mix them. Keep body text at a size
that survives projection. Put detail in the speaker notes rather than shrinking
the slide font.

## Speaker notes

Write notes as full sentences. A deck that cannot be presented by someone else
is not finished.
