# Charting

The chart type follows the question, not the other way round.

## Choosing a form

- Comparison across categories: bar chart.
- Change over time: line chart.
- Distribution: histogram or box plot.
- Relationship between two measures: scatter plot.

## Honest axes

Start a bar chart's value axis at zero. A truncated axis on a bar chart
misstates the comparison it is drawn to make.

## Labels and colour

Label the axes with units. Do not rely on colour alone to distinguish series,
and check the palette against a colour-vision simulation.
