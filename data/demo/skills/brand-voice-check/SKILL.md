# Brand voice check

Check the copy against the guidelines that exist, and say so when none do.

## What to check

- Product and feature names, capitalised exactly as the guidelines specify.
- Reading level against the stated target.
- Tone: the guidelines usually name what to avoid as well as what to aim for.
- Claims that need legal or factual support.

## How to report

Quote the original line, give the suggested rewrite, and name the guideline it
follows. A suggestion without a reason is just a preference.
